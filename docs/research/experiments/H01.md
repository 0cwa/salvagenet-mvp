# H01 experiment record

## Selected MVP semantics

- Controller-to-host uploads are separate resources from public HTTPS imports.
- Uploads are sequential, resumable, and exact-replay idempotent.
- Chunks are limited to 1 MiB and carry their own SHA-256.
- Progress metadata and payload bytes are fsynced and stored under app-private staging paths.
- Completion verifies the whole expected size and digest before atomically publishing the existing artifact manifest/payload layout.
- Cancellation never deletes a completed/active artifact.
- The hardened public URL importer and its SSRF/DNS-rebinding policy remain unchanged.

## Deferred

Parallel/out-of-order chunk upload, TUF, OCI transport, and distributed upload mirrors are not required for the one-phone MVP.
